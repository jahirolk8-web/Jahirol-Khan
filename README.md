INT. COFFEE SHOP - NIGHT
anubhab (30-er kacha-kachi), ekta purano notebook-e kichu likhche. Baire mukhol dharay brishti porche. Coffee cup theke dhowa uthche.
Door bell peye Anubhab matha tule takalo. EKTA SHADOW / NISHAT (28) chhata bondho kore bhetore dhuklo. Nishat-er poron-e ojana rastar kado aache.
NISHAT
(Dhire dhire Anubhab-er tebile giye bose)
Tumi ekhono ekhane bose aacho? Shomoy je shesh hoye geche, Anubhab.
ANUBHAB
(Notebook ta bondho kare)
Shomoy kokhono shesh hoy na, Nishat. Shudhu amra thakbo ki thakbo na, sheta amra thik kori.
NISHAT
(Gombhir gola)
Tumi jano ora tomake khujche? Shob shotto jene jawar por ora tomake r bachhte debe na.
ANUBHAB
(Halka hese coffee-te chumuk dey)
Shotto ta to amar kache aache. Ora amake marte pare, kintu ei lekha ta muche phelte parbe na.
Anubhab tebil-er opor ekta Kalo Envelope egiye dey Nishat-er dike.
ANUBHAB (CONT'D)
Ei ta nie jao. Shoker shohor ta ke bolo, shob shotto ekhane aache.
Nishat envelope-ta haate ney. Haat kapche tar. Outside, ekta gari sroshe thambhar shobdo shona jay. Headlight-er alo glass-er bhetor diye Anubhab-er mukh-e pore.
NISHAT
(Voy peye)
Ora eshe geche!
ANUBHAB
(Shanto bhabe)
Tumi pichon-er dorja diye beriye jao. Ami eder ekkhan-e atke rakhchi.
